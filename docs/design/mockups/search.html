mockup Group order is by the best hit in each group, not a fixed type order, and it is frozen for the life of the query. The per-group cap is a 40-row budget divided by the six groups that have hits, so six rows each, and a truncated group's last row carries the real total. A group with no hits does not render at all: there is no empty Music heading on a query that found no music.

mockup The same rules, two groups. The cap is the 40-row budget divided by the two groups with hits — ten each — so neither group is truncated and no Show all row is needed; the full stack, which splits the same budget six ways, truncates two of its groups. A group with no hits does not render at all, so the four types with no catalogue source produce no headings here rather than four empty ones — and Home says why, which is the part a search screen cannot.

mockup Dune is one row rather than four. The novel, its M4B edition and the 2021 film are one linked work, so it renders in the group of its best-scoring medium with the other media as chips on the title. Cross-media linking itself is v0.3; the rendering rule is drawn here so the six-type search can be reviewed against it.
